Hello, world!

I’m Quenifer—and I think in diagrams.

I’m fascinated by the invisible connections between the way we think, behave, work, and relate to one another. When life feels complicated, I turn those connections into visual systems that make problems easier to understand.

What began as a way to navigate my own life has become the way I approach everything—from personal growth to creativity and my career.

The method behind the diagrams

How my brain works

I turn messy, abstract problems into systems people can see. My ideas usually come from three ways of thinking:

01. Idea synthesis

I connect insights from psychology, business, philosophy, design, and lived experience to create new frameworks.

02. Lateral thinking

I look beyond the obvious answer, reframing a problem until a different path becomes visible.

03. Associative thinking

I notice connections between ideas that seem unrelated—and use those patterns to uncover what others might miss.

Why I’m building Qthos

I want to help people make sense of their lives.

As someone with ADHD, I’ve always had a mind that moves between ideas quickly. These mini systems began as a way to organize my own thoughts.

Now, they guide the stories and frameworks I share as we navigate this big, beautiful life together.
